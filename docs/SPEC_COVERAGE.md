## Part 1: library, support, concepts, diagnostics, memory, metaprogramming, utilities

Audit of 2026-10-07 against the draft at eel.is (revision `c7015b48` of github.com/Eelis/draft),
GCC 16.2 and Clang 23.1. Clauses: [library] (the header tables, [compliance], [version.syn]),
[support], [concepts], [diagnostics], [mem], [meta] (with reflection, `<meta>`), [utilities].
`<charconv>` is in [text] (28.2) in this draft and `<stdckdint.h>` in [numerics]: not part of
this range.

### How to re-run

```sh
tools/spec_audit/part1/draft_tables.py --html full.html   # data/version.tsv, data/headers.tsv
tools/spec_audit/part1/inventory.py --html full.html      # data/entities.tsv
tools/spec_audit/part1/run.py [-c gcc|clang] [--summary counts.tsv] [probe...]
tools/spec_audit/part1/specifiers.py --html full.html     # constexpr/noexcept screening
```

(`full.html` is a saved https://eel.is/c++draft/full; without `--html` the scripts download it.)
`run.py` compiles everything with `-fsyntax-only` against `include/` (needs `build/<cc>` for the
generated headers) and exits 0 when nothing fails unexpectedly. Its checks:

- **headers**: every header of Tables 24, 25 ([headers]) and 47 ([support.c.headers]) compiles;
  every header of Table 27 ([compliance]) also with `-ffreestanding -fno-exceptions -fno-rtti`.
- **version**: all 313 macros of [version.syn] with the draft's value, in `<version>` and in each
  header the synopsis names (/2); the 162 freestanding ones in a freestanding `<version>`; the 19
  of /3 with `-DYCXX_HARDENED=1` and not without it (/7).
- **names**: every entity the code blocks of [support] .. [utilities] declare
  (`data/entities.tsv`, generated by `inventory.py` with `tools/gen_draft_names.py`'s tokenizer):
  a using-declaration per namespace-scope name, a derived class's using-declaration per member
  (`data/samples.tsv` gives the specialization), `#ifndef` per macro; the freestanding items
  ([freestanding.item]/4-5: the synopsis comments) again in a freestanding compilation.
- **probes**: `tools/spec_audit/part1/probes/<stable.name>.cpp`, hand-written per subclause:
  signatures and return types, `constexpr` (evaluated in `static_assert`), `noexcept`, constraints
  (a call that must be rejected, through a concept), deduction guides, and for the C++26 additions
  their basic semantics.
- Known failures are listed in `data/expected.tsv` (tables) and as `// GAP:` lines (probes), each
  naming a gap below; a known failure that no longer happens is reported (STALE / XPASS).

`specifiers.py` compares, per function name, the constexpr and noexcept overloads of the draft
with libycxx's declarations; its 14 candidates were all overload-counting noise (checked by hand
and by the probes).

### Gap list

Classes: (1) missing entity, (2) wrong shape, (3) missing or wrong behaviour, (4) feature macro,
(5) intentional (STATUS/DECISIONS), (6) blocked by the compiler, (7) draft defect.

- **P1-01 (1, 6) [ptrtag]: `pointer_tag_pair` is not implemented.** Missing: `max_pointer_bits_available`,
  `pointer_bits_available` ([ptrtag.bits]), class template `pointer_tag_pair` with its deduction
  guides ([ptrtag.pair.general]), its `tuple_size`/`tuple_element` specializations and `get`
  ([memory.syn], [ptrtag.pair.get]), and `__cpp_lib_pointer_tag_pair` (202606L, freestanding).
  STATUS lists it under "Known limitations" (`<memory>`: "no `pointer_tag_pair`"), not as a
  deliberate omission. Effort: the run-time part (tag in the low alignment bits, `tagged_pointer`
  / `from_tagged`, comparisons, tuple protocol; freestanding) is about a day with tests. The
  constructors, `from_overaligned`, `pointer()`, `tag()`, `swap` and the comparisons are constexpr
  with "Constant When: preconditions are met" ([ptrtag.pair.cons]/2, [ptrtag.pair.overalign]/1):
  in constant evaluation a tag has to live in the pointer's unused bits of a `sizeof(Ptr)` object,
  which neither GCC 16.2 nor Clang 23.1 can evaluate (no pointer-tagging builtin; Clang's
  `__builtin_align_down` cannot set bits). A conforming constexpr part is blocked on the compilers.
- **P1-02 (1) [memory.syn]: `pmr::indirect` and `pmr::polymorphic` were missing.** Fixed
  (968fb44, std module c881689); test `indirect/pmr_aliases.pass.cpp`.
- **P1-03 (2) [meta.type.synop]: `is_applicable`, `is_nothrow_applicable`, `apply_result`
  (`_v`, `_t`) were declared only through the tuple-protocol headers, not by `<type_traits>`.**
  Fixed (03628bb); test `type_traits/is_applicable_header.compile.pass.cpp`.
- **P1-04 (4) [version.syn]/2: `__cpp_lib_stdbit_h` and `__cpp_lib_stdckdint_h` were not defined
  by `<stdbit.h>` / `<stdckdint.h>`.** Fixed (6e06cc1); tests `version/header_stdbit_h`,
  `version/header_stdckdint_h`.
- **P1-05 (4) [version.syn]/5: `__cpp_lib_replaceable_contract_violation_handler` was not
  defined.** Fixed (eda75ce): 202603L with the compiler's contracts (the runtime's default
  `::handle_contract_violation` is replaceable), 0 otherwise; test
  `version/replaceable_contract_violation_handler`.
- **P1-06 (4) [version.syn]/3, /7: `__cpp_lib_hardened_shared_ptr_array` and
  `__cpp_lib_hardened_basic_stacktrace` were defined without `YCXX_HARDENED`;
  `__cpp_lib_hardened_view_interface` was missing although `view_interface::front`/`back` check
  their hardened preconditions.** Fixed (16936e8); tests `version/hardened_macros`,
  `version/hardened_macros_absent`.
- **P1-07 (3, 4) [counted.iter.*], [common.iter.*]: hardened preconditions are checked only in
  part, so `__cpp_lib_hardened_counted_iterator` / `__cpp_lib_hardened_common_iterator` (202506L)
  are not defined.** `counted_iterator` checks only its constructor (`n >= 0`); missing:
  `operator*` (/elem: `length > 0`), `operator[]` (`n < length`), `++`, `++(int)`, `+=`
  (`n <= length`), `-=` (`-n <= length`), `iter_move`, `iter_swap`
  (`include/ycxx/core/iterator_adaptors.hpp` 891-1006). `common_iterator` checks `*`, `->`, `++`;
  missing: the converting constructor and assignment (`!x.v_.valueless_by_exception()`), `==` and
  `-` (both operands not valueless), `iter_move` and `iter_swap` (`holds_alternative<I>`)
  (same file, 1083-1238). Small (an hour with death tests in `tests/ycxx/precondition`); the
  subclauses belong to [iterators], so it is left to that part of the audit to avoid conflicting
  edits.
- **P1-08 (4) [version.syn]: `__cpp_lib_view_interface` (202606L, `view_interface::at`) was not
  defined although `at` is implemented and tested.** Fixed (786142e); test
  `version/view_interface`.
- **P1-09 (6) Blocked by the compilers** (STATUS, "Known compiler gaps"; the library side is in
  place where the compiler allows it):
  - Clang 23.1: no reflection (`<meta>` empty: 260 names, `__cpp_lib_reflection`,
    `__cpp_lib_define_static`), no `__builtin_is_structural` (`is_structural`,
    `__cpp_lib_is_structural`), no contracts (`__cpp_lib_contracts`; `<contracts>` only declares),
    no throwing during constant evaluation (`__cpp_lib_constexpr_exceptions` in 7 headers), no
    `__builtin_is_pointer_interconvertible_with_class` (`__cpp_lib_is_pointer_interconvertible`;
    `is_pointer_interconvertible_with_class` and `is_corresponding_member` are declared).
  - GCC 16.2: no `__builtin_is_within_lifetime` (`__cpp_lib_is_within_lifetime`,
    `__cpp_lib_start_lifetime`); `<meta>` needs `-freflection` (without it, `<version>` does not
    define `__cpp_lib_reflection`/`__cpp_lib_define_static`; with it, all 260 `std::meta` names and
    the probe `meta.reflection.cpp` pass).
- **P1-10 (5) [expected.object.eq]: `operator==(const expected&, const T2&)` deduces its left
  operand** (STATUS, "Deliberate divergences"). Removed features (`auto_ptr`, `result_of`, ...) are
  deliberate omissions (STATUS); STATUS lists every Annex D entity as provided (not re-probed here).

No other gaps were found: every probed entity of [support], [concepts], [diagnostics], [mem]
(outside [ptrtag]), [meta] and [utilities] exists in the right header, with the probed shape, and
the C++26 additions probed (constant_wrapper, function_ref with constant_wrapper,
copyable_function, bind_front/bind_back/not_fn with a constant callable, optional<T&> and its
range support, constrained comparisons, tuple-like integer_sequence, apply with
is_applicable, exception_ptr_cast returning optional<const E&>, constexpr shared_ptr/weak_ptr,
indirect/polymorphic, owner_hash/owner_equal, type_order, the bit permutations and shifts,
`view_interface::at`, C23 `va_start`) behave as specified in constant evaluation. There are no
TODO/FIXME markers in `include/` or `src/`.

### Draft defects noticed

- [ptrtag.bits]/2 names `tagged_pointer_pair` and `tp.tagged()`; the class is `pointer_tag_pair`
  and the member `tagged_pointer()`.
- [ptrtag.pair.tagops]/3 twice names `pointer_tag_type` (meant: `pointer_tag_pair`), and /2 uses
  `ptr` and `tag`, defined nowhere (meant: `pointer()` and `tag()` of `*this`).
- [ptrtag.pair.general]: the deduction guide `pointer_tag_pair(Ptr*) -> pointer_tag_pair<Ptr*>`
  has no constructor taking a pointer alone (the constructors take `(U* p, tag_type t)`), so a
  deduction through it always fails overload resolution afterwards.
